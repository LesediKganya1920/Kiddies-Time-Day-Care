About Us

A second home for your little one

We're an English-medium pre-school welcoming children from 2 to 6 years old. Our days are full of play-based learning, songs, story time and plenty of outdoor fun — all in a safe, nurturing space run by carers who know every child by name.

Our Day

What a day at Kiddies Time looks like

Structure and play go hand in hand, so every day feels exciting but never overwhelming.

Morning

Drop-off & Breakfast

Children settle in with a warm welcome and breakfast for those on our meal plan.

Mid-morning

Learning & Play

Age-appropriate activities, songs, stories and creative play in English.

Midday

Lunch Time

A shared lunch break — either from our kitchen or your child's own lunch box.

Afternoon

Outdoor & Rest Time

Fresh air, outdoor play and a quiet rest before home time.
